Frequently asked questions

Which photos work best?

A sharp, well-lit photo where the face fills most of the frame, against a plain background. One subject per photo.

What does Pro unlock?

Previewing your photo as a finished cross stitch is free. Pro unlocks the pattern itself: the full chart with symbols, the exact DMC codes and skein counts, stitch tracking with a session timer, and the printable PDF chart.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Kindred Stitch, go to Settings, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Kindred Stitch. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next year.

Which fabric and threads do I need?

Aida fabric in the count you chose (14-count suits most beginners), a tapestry needle, an embroidery hoop and the DMC six-strand floss listed with your pattern, stitched with 2 strands.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Every photo is processed on your iPhone. See our privacy policy.
