Kindred Stitch

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: your photos never leave your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Kindred Stitch is published by Abdelilah Cherkaoui. Contact: cherkaoui.abdo@gmail.com.

What the app does with your data

  • Photos. The photos you choose, or take with the camera, are turned into a pattern on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device tools to lift the subject off the background. No photo is uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Patterns, progress and threads. Your patterns, stitching progress, the threads you own and your answers to the introduction stay on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. To know whether your Pro access is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history, not your name or email. We also use RevenueCat's sales statistics (for example, how many people subscribe), which are not linked to you (RevenueCat privacy policy).

What we don't do

We use no advertising, no ad tracking and no analytics of how you use the app. We neither sell nor share any data.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For any question, write to cherkaoui.abdo@gmail.com. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from them.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with its date.
